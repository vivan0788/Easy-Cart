Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Frontend Demonstration Notice: VivanX Shop is a prototype e-commerce application. All cart data and order tracking are managed purely client-side via your browser's localStorage. No sensitive credit card details or server-side telemetry are collected.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VivanX Shop ("we," "our," or "us"). We value your trust and are committed to safeguarding your personal data in accordance with modern data privacy frameworks including GDPR and CCPA.

2. Information We Collect

When you browse or interact with VivanX Shop, we may collect:

  • Device Information: Browser type, viewport dimensions, language preferences.
  • Voluntary Form Submissions: Full name, email address, phone number, and support inquiry messages submitted through our contact forms.
  • Local Cart Storage: Items added to cart and promo codes are stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

Collected data is used strictly to provide you with an interactive shopping interface, process customer inquiries, preserve cart items across page refreshes, and test user experience ergonomics.

4. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

We utilize client-side localStorage to remember items in your shopping bag and recently viewed preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard best practices, SSL/TLS transport layer security, and code integrity audits to protect any client interaction against unauthorized tampering.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes trustworthy CDNs such as Tailwind CSS, Font Awesome, and Google Fonts to deliver styling and iconography. These providers may process standard HTTP headers.

7. User Rights

Under international privacy regulations, you have the right to inspect, export, or erase any personal information associated with your browsing session. You may clear your browser's site cache at any time to remove all saved cart information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our storefront does not knowingly market to or solicit personally identifiable data from individuals under 13 years of age. If you believe a minor has provided information, please reach out to us for immediate remediation.

9. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect UI enhancements or regulatory requirements. Any adjustments will be indicated with an updated "Last Modified" timestamp at the top of this document.
